# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CaseView to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience level, background or identity. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people reporting problems.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues, and give enough detail to reproduce a problem.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers privately, using the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or GitHub's *Report content* feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they find harmful.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
